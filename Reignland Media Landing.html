What is FPV — and why does it outperform a normal venue video?

Standard drone shots circle a building from the sky. FPV is different: the camera flies inside your business at eye level, moving like a guest who never stops. Viewers watch to the end because it feels like being there — which is exactly what a venue tour, listing walkthrough or event recap needs.

Perfect for

  • Breweries, cafés, restaurants & venue tours
  • Real-estate listings and display homes
  • Events, festivals & action shots close to the subject
  • Construction & developer progress storytelling
  • Vertical clips cut for TikTok, Reels & Shorts

  • Fast turnaround — footage delivered in as little as 24h
  • Standard aerials, 360° panoramas & night ops available

Before you book any FPV operator, ask two questions.

In Australia, flying FPV commercially — goggles on, drone beyond the pilot's direct line of sight — requires a CASA approval (EVLOS). Most people selling cheap fly-throughs don't hold one. If an uninsured, unapproved flight goes wrong inside your venue, that risk lands on your business, not theirs.

Do you hold a CASA EVLOS approval for commercial FPV? We do. Reignland Media operates under a CASA ReOC with EVLOS approval.
Are you insured for commercial FPV work? We are — full public liability cover on every job.
