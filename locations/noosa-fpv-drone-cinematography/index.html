faq

Drone services Noosa — frequently asked questions

How much do drone services cost in Noosa?

FPV fly-through tours start at $495. Standard drone photography and aerial video are quoted per job depending on location, access and deliverables. Every project is priced up front with no surprises.

What areas of Noosa do you cover?

Noosa Heads, Sunshine Beach, Noosaville, Tewantin, Cooroy, Peregian, Cootharaba and the entire Noosa region. We also service North Brisbane, the Sunshine Coast, Gympie, the Fraser Coast and Bundaberg.

What drone services do you provide?

FPV fly-through tours of resorts and venues, standard drone photography and aerial video for real estate and tourism marketing, site progress and roof inspection imagery, and ReOC-approved night operations.

Are you licensed to fly a drone commercially in Noosa?

Yes. We hold a CASA Remote Operator Certificate (ReOC) and an EVLOS approval, which is the legal requirement for commercial outdoor FPV flying in Australia. Many operators do not hold one.

How fast can I get the footage?

Most projects are delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Rush turnaround is available on request.
