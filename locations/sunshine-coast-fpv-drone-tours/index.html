faq

Drone services Sunshine Coast — frequently asked questions

How much do drone services cost on the Sunshine Coast?

FPV fly-through tours start at $495. Standard drone photography and aerial video are quoted per job depending on location, access and deliverables. Every project is priced up front with no surprises.

What areas of the Sunshine Coast do you cover?

Caloundra through to Noosa, including hinterland venues and properties. Our council permit covers 4:30am to 10pm. We also work across North Brisbane, Gympie, the Fraser Coast and Bundaberg.

What drone services do you provide?

FPV fly-through tours of venues and properties, standard drone photography and aerial video for real estate listings and marketing, site progress and roof inspection imagery, and ReOC-approved night operations.

Are you licensed to fly a drone commercially in Queensland?

Yes. We hold a CASA Remote Operator Certificate (ReOC) and an EVLOS approval, which is the legal requirement for commercial outdoor FPV flying in Australia. Many operators do not hold one.

How fast can I get the footage?

Most projects are delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Rush turnaround is available on request.
