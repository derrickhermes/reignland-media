faq

Drone services Brisbane — frequently asked questions

How much do drone services cost in Brisbane?

FPV fly-through tours start at $495. Standard drone photography and aerial video are quoted per job depending on location, access and deliverables. Every project is priced up front with no surprises.

What areas of Brisbane do you cover?

Brisbane CBD, North Brisbane suburbs (Chermside, Aspley, Strathpine), Western suburbs (Kenmore, Indooroopilly), and the entire Greater Brisbane region. We also service the Sunshine Coast, Gympie, the Fraser Coast and Bundaberg.

What drone services do you provide?

FPV fly-through tours of commercial facilities and venues, standard drone photography and aerial video for real estate listings and marketing, site progress and roof inspection imagery, and ReOC-approved night operations.

Are you licensed to fly a drone commercially in Brisbane?

Yes. We hold a CASA Remote Operator Certificate (ReOC) and an EVLOS approval, which is the legal requirement for commercial outdoor FPV flying in Australia. Many operators do not hold one.

How fast can I get the footage?

Most projects are delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Rush turnaround is available on request.
