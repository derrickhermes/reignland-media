Trust & licensing

CASA-approved · Fully insured

Commercial FPV flying in Australia requires a CASA EVLOS approval. We hold one — so your venue, your guests and your insurance are never at risk.

CASA ReOC operator EVLOS-approved FPV Public liability insured SCC operating permit Night ops approved

Before booking any FPV operator, ask two questions:

“Do you hold a CASA EVLOS approval for commercial FPV?” — We do.

“Are you insured for commercial FPV work?” — Yes.
